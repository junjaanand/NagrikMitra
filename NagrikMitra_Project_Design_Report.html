2. Abstract

NagrikMitra (नागरिक मित्र — "Citizen's Friend") is an AI-powered, full-stack civic technology web application designed to empower Indian citizens to discover government welfare schemes they are eligible for, understand complex application procedures, and receive document-level guidance through a conversational, multilingual, and voice-enabled interface.

Problem Being Solved: India offers over 4,700 central and state government welfare schemes, yet millions of deserving citizens—particularly rural, elderly, and low-literacy populations—fail to access benefits due to information fragmentation across hundreds of departmental portals, intricate overlapping eligibility criteria (spanning age, income, caste, domicile, land holding, BPL status, and disability), legalistic language, and the lack of multilingual and voice accessibility. Existing government portals (such as myScheme.gov.in) provide static directories and basic search, but lack deterministic personalization, profile persistence, real-time voice interaction, and intelligent document preparation.

Proposed Solution: NagrikMitra implements a hybrid architectural paradigm that unifies a deterministic rule engine with a Large Language Model (LLM) and multimodal AI. The rule engine performs exact, hallucination-free eligibility evaluations against 4,693 curated schemes using 9 structured parameters. The LLM (Google Gemini) powers an interactive conversational chatbot that extracts citizen profiles through natural dialogue in Hindi and English, answers contextual queries via Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over ChromaDB vector embeddings, and supports bidirectional voice interaction using the Web Speech API. The system incorporates Optical Character Recognition (Tesseract OCR and live device camera scanning) to auto-extract demographic data from identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Ration Card), alongside secure user authentication (Firebase Auth with Google One-Click Login and Phone OTP) for profile and bookmark persistence, dynamic scheme comparison, and animated visual analytics.

Expected Outcome: A responsive, high-performance web application featuring a public landing page with interactive statistics, a secure citizen dashboard, animated eligibility analytics, a voice-enabled conversational assistant, and an automated document readiness evaluator. The system targets ≥85% scheme matching precision, ≥90% entity extraction accuracy, ≥80% OCR field accuracy, and sub-5-second response times, demonstrating how modern AI and accessible design can bridge India's welfare delivery gap.

3. Introduction

3.1 Background of the Project

Under the Digital India initiative and various central and state social protection mandates, India maintains one of the world's most extensive social safety nets. Official welfare aggregators, most notably the myScheme national portal developed by the Digital India Corporation under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), catalogue more than 4,700 schemes implemented across all 37 states and union territories.

These programs span vital socio-economic sectors: direct income support for farmers (PM-KISAN), universal health insurance (Ayushman Bharat - PMJAY), subsidized rural and urban housing (PM Awas Yojana), maternity and child development benefits, educational scholarships, and micro-credit financing (PM Mudra Yojana).

Despite the substantial fiscal commitment, welfare distribution faces the "last-mile awareness challenge." Significant portions of eligible populations remain excluded because administrative rules are complex, eligibility criteria overlap in subtle ways, and citizens do not know where or how to apply.

3.2 Problem Context

  1. Discovery and Dimensional Complexity: With 4,693+ schemes across 20 functional categories and 37 geographic jurisdictions, citizens cannot manually identify relevant schemes. Eligibility is a multi-dimensional intersection of age, gender, caste category, annual income ceiling, rural/urban classification, land holding, BPL classification, and disability status.
  2. Linguistic and Literacy Barriers: Official administrative documents are drafted in legalistic English or standard Hindi terminology. Millions of rural citizens who communicate orally or in colloquial dialects cannot interpret eligibility clauses or required procedural steps.
  3. Information Dispersion: Central and state schemes are managed by distinct ministries, state departments, and directorates. A farmer seeking crop insurance, tractor subsidies, and family health coverage must navigate three separate portals.
  4. Repetitive Document Hurdles: Citizens frequently visit administrative centres (Gram Panchayat offices, Taluk offices, Common Service Centres) only to be turned away for missing one specific certificate. No integrated mechanism calculates a citizen's document preparedness before application.
  5. Lack of Personalization and Persistence: Existing portals require users to re-enter demographic details on every visit. Without secure authentication and profile persistence, citizens cannot track previously discovered schemes, monitor document readiness, or receive tailored updates.

3.3 Why This Project is Needed

NagrikMitra addresses these systemic gaps by creating an intelligent digital companion that pairs mathematical rule precision with conversational AI. By introducing voice interaction, live camera OCR, Google one-click authentication, and visual dashboard analytics, NagrikMitra bridges the digital divide for marginalized citizens while providing an engaging, modern platform for student researchers and civic tech practitioners.

4. Problem Statement

To design and develop a full-stack, AI-powered citizen services web application that enables Indian citizens to discover personalized government welfare schemes based on their demographic profile, through a multilingual conversational voice interface and live document scanning, utilizing a hybrid rule-engine and LLM architecture that guarantees deterministic, hallucination-free eligibility evaluations across 4,693+ central and state schemes with secure user profile persistence.

Key technical challenges addressed:

  • Preventing LLM hallucinations in statutory eligibility determination by decoupling conversational dialogue from logical rule evaluation.
  • Supporting multilingual and voice-driven interactions for non-English and low-literacy users.
  • Automated extraction and validation of identity attributes directly from physical document images via OCR.
  • Delivering a high-impact, accessible user experience featuring instant Google authentication, animated analytics, and side-by-side scheme comparison.

10. Module Description

Module 1: Deterministic Eligibility Engine (Rule Engine)

  • Objective: Evaluate user demographics against 4,693 government welfare schemes with mathematical certainty and zero hallucination.
  • Input Parameters: Age ($A$), Gender ($G$), State ($S$), Caste ($C$), Annual Income ($I$), Residence ($R$), BPL Status ($B$), Disability Status ($D$), Occupation ($O$).
  • Logic Formulation: A scheme $k$ is marked eligible ($\text{Eligible}_k = 1$) if and only if: $$\text{Eligible}_k = \left( A \ge \text{MinAge}_k \land A \le \text{MaxAge}_k \right) \land \left( \text{Gender}_k \in {G, \text{"all"}} \right) \land \left( C \in \text{CasteList}_k \right) \land \left( I \le \text{MaxIncome}_k \right) \land \left( \text{Res}_k \in {R, \text{"both"}} \right) \land \left( S \in \text{StateList}_k \lor \text{StateList}_k = {\text{"All"}} \right) \land \left( \neg \text{BPL}_k \lor B \right) \land \left( \neg \text{Disability}_k \lor D \right)$$
  • Output: Filtered, categorized list of schemes with direct applicability tags.

Module 2: AI Voice & Chat Companion (Gemini + RAG + Web Speech API)

  • Objective: Provide a sympathetic, bilingual (Hindi/English) voice assistant capable of extracting profile data and explaining bureaucratic procedures.
  • Voice Ingestion: Web Speech Recognition API streams spoken Hindi audio directly into text on the client device at zero latency and zero external cost.
  • RAG Execution: LangChain embeds user queries, computes cosine similarity against ChromaDB chunks of scheme guidelines, and constructs a grounded prompt for the Gemini 1.5 Flash model.
  • Speech Synthesis: Answers are synthesized back into spoken Hindi/English audio via SpeechSynthesis API.

Module 3: Document Scanner & Identity Vision (OCR)

  • Objective: Eliminate manual typing by digitizing identity documents via image upload or direct camera streams.
  • Pipeline: Image capture $\rightarrow$ Grayscale conversion $\rightarrow$ Adaptive thresholding (Otsu's binarization) $\rightarrow$ Tesseract 5.0 LSTM text recognition $\rightarrow$ Pattern matching.
  • Target Patterns: 12-digit Aadhaar UID (^\d{4}\s\d{4}\s\d{4}$), 10-character alphanumeric PAN (^[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}$), Date of Birth (\d{2}/\d{2}/\d{4}).
  • Outcome: One-click pre-filling of the citizen's eligibility profile.

Module 4: Authentication & User Profile Management (Firebase Auth)

  • Objective: Provide frictionless, secure login and maintain persistent state for citizens.
  • Mechanisms:
    1. Google One-Click OAuth 2.0 Sign-In.
    2. Phone Number + SMS OTP for rural citizens without active email addresses.
    3. Firebase JWT Bearer Token validation in FastAPI middleware.
  • Capabilities: Persistent storage of demographic profiles, custom notes, bookmarked schemes, and document upload history.

Module 5: Scheme Directory Explorer & Comparison Engine

  • Objective: Enable structured search and side-by-side analytical evaluation of multiple welfare options.
  • Features:
    • Real-time full-text search across scheme titles, ministries, and departments.
    • Multi-faceted filters for 37 States and 20 welfare sectors.
    • Multi-scheme comparison matrix contrasting benefit amounts, eligibility restrictions, processing duration, and document checklists.

Module 6: Application Assistant & Document Readiness Evaluator

  • Objective: Ensure citizens do not fail during in-person office visits due to documentation deficiencies.
  • Calculations: Analyzes documents_required string tokens against user-declared or scanned documents: $$\text{Readiness Score} = \left( \frac{\text{Documents Verified}}{\text{Total Documents Required}} \right) \times 100%$$
  • Outputs: Color-coded progress bar, missing document warnings, actionable preparation steps, and direct portal redirection URLs.

Module 7: Interactive Analytics Dashboard

  • Objective: Present macro-level welfare opportunities visually to inspire user confidence.
  • Visual Elements: Animated Recharts bar charts showing schemes by department, pie charts illustrating funding and subsidy distributions, and QR code generators allowing citizens to carry their personalized eligibility report on mobile devices or hand off to CSC operators.

11.2 Algorithms & Mathematical Techniques

Technique Pipeline Location Formal Purpose
Predicate Calculus Filtering Eligibility Matcher Evaluates conjunctive multi-criteria predicates against relational records in $O(N)$ time.
Vector Space Cosine Similarity RAG Document Retrieval Computes angle between query vector $\vec{q}$ and document chunk $\vec{d}$: $\cos(\theta) = \frac{\vec{q} \cdot \vec{d}}{|\vec{q}| |\vec{d}|}$.
Named Entity Extraction (Few-Shot) Gemini Chat Processor Parses unstructured vernacular utterances into structured JSON key-value pairs.
LSTM Recurrent Neural OCR Tesseract Vision Engine Character sequence probability estimation over line-segmented image slices.
Otsu Binarization Algorithm Image Preprocessor Minimizes intra-class variance of black/white pixels for optimal document contrast.
HMAC-SHA256 Token Verification FastAPI Security Guard Verifies cryptographic signature of Firebase JWT bearer tokens.

15. Limitations

  1. Static Dataset Temporal Snapshot: The system utilizes the curated July 2026 dataset (4,693 schemes). Newly announced state budgetary modifications require periodic dataset re-ingestion.
  2. Absence of Direct UIDAI e-KYC Integration: Due to statutory licensing constraints surrounding the Aadhaar Act, document scanning performs optical text extraction rather than cryptographic biometric validation against UIDAI central servers.
  3. Connectivity Prerequisite for Generative AI: While the deterministic rule engine and database can run on local intranet or edge servers, the Gemini conversational assistant requires active internet connectivity.
  4. Varying OCR Reliability on Damaged Physical Cards: Handwritten, laminated, or worn ration cards exhibit reduced character recognition accuracy under low ambient lighting.
  5. External Application Redirection: NagrikMitra prepares the citizen, organizes paperwork, and verifies eligibility, but final application submission occurs on the respective government department portals.

16. Future Scope

  1. Direct API Setu & UMANG Gateway Integration: Direct statutory API handshakes to submit forms and track application lifecycle stages in real-time.
  2. Autonomous Web Scraping & Ingestion Engine: Periodic crawlers running against myscheme.gov.in to continuously flag revised guidelines or budgetary allocations.
  3. Dialect-Level Regional Voice Models: Integration of Bhashini (Government of India AI initiative) for 22 scheduled regional languages including Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, and Kannada.
  4. Full Offline Edge Mode (PWA): Lightweight client-side database caching allowing citizens in zero-connectivity remote regions to evaluate basic eligibility.
  5. Civic Grievance Redressal Integration: Expansion into automated civic complaint drafting (water, electricity, roads) submitted directly to state portals like CPGRAMS.
  6. DigiLocker Integration: Direct consent-based document fetching from national DigiLocker repositories, eliminating manual document scanning entirely.

17. Conclusion

NagrikMitra (नागरिक मित्र) represents a transformative, technologically rigorous intervention in Indian civic technology. By reconciling the discovery gap across 4,693 central and state welfare initiatives, the platform bridges the divide between government welfare resources and the citizens who need them most.

The project’s critical innovation is its hybrid engineering methodology: by pairing a deterministic rule engine (ensuring absolute correctness) with generative conversational intelligence (ensuring multilingual empathy and accessibility), NagrikMitra eliminates the hallucination risks common to generative models in regulatory domains.

Enhanced by accessible user authentication (Firebase Google & Phone login), voice interaction via the Web Speech API, automated document digitization via Tesseract OCR, animated visual analytics, and side-by-side scheme comparison, NagrikMitra establishes an exemplary benchmark for final-year engineering projects—delivering high academic depth, technological sophistication, and profound social impact.
